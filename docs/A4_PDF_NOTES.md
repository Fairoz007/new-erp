# A4 PDF notes (docs/A4_PDF_PROMPT.md)

Running log of the A4 letterhead format work: what changed, what was decided, what is verified and
what is not. One section per stage.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every printed and downloaded document on the full letterhead, reports and lists included | Requested by the owner |
| 2 | Portrait always; wide tables shrink (not below 6.5 pt) and wrap; landscape only above 10 columns | Applied in stage 2 |
| 3 | Print gives the same pages as Download PDF | Requested by the owner; lists (stage 2) and statements (stage 3) done |
| 4 | CSV exports unchanged | Suggested |
| 5 | Payslip bank account shows the last 4 digits only | Applied in stage 4 |
| 6 | Corner shows the document number; reports show the period | Suggested |

## Open points

- List filters chosen with a page's own filter controls (status, category drop-downs and similar) are
  not named on the PDF yet: `DataTable` only knows its search text, and Reports passes office and
  dates. The rows are still the filtered rows. Naming every page's filters would mean wiring each
  page; left for the owner to decide.
- "PURCHASE BILL" now prints at 12.5 pt instead of 13 pt in the PDF corner (it ran 0.4 mm into the
  space beside the logo at 13 pt). The on-screen/print view of purchase bills is unchanged until it
  moves to the PDF print in a later stage.

## Stage 1: One letterhead frame

- **New `src/lib/a4/frame.ts`**, the frame every document will use:
  - `startA4` (A4 portrait document with the frame on page 1), `drawFrame`, `addFramedPage`,
    `ensureSpace` (move a block to a new framed page when it does not fit) and `stampPages`
    (`<label> · Page x of y` on every page, drawn last);
  - page-1 helpers `sellerLine`/`sellerBlock`, `textBlock` and `factsBlock`;
  - `a4Table`: the safe-area margins, header on every page, rows never split, the frame on every
    new page, and `numeric` columns right-aligned with their headings;
  - closing blocks `totalsBlock` (totals plus amount in words, notes, terms) and
    `bankAndSignatures` (bank details when there are any, "Prepared by" and "Authorised signature &
    stamp");
  - output: `savePdf` (a number becomes `<number>.pdf`, a full `.pdf` name is kept), `printPdf`
    and `loadLetterheadBytes`.
- **`printPdf`** prints the generated PDF itself from a hidden frame, so paper matches the file. If
  the browser cannot print from the frame (it throws, or the frame does not load within 10 s, as on
  some phones), the file is downloaded instead. It resolves to `printed` or `downloaded` so the
  caller can say which happened. Not yet wired to any button; stages 2 to 5 use it.
- **`src/lib/letterheadPdf.ts`** (invoice, credit/debit note, purchase bill, pending-bills
  statement) now draws through the frame. Its exports are unchanged (`buildInvoicePdf`,
  `buildStatementPdf`, `loadLetterheadBytes`, `savePdf`, plus `printPdf`), so no caller changed.
- **No visible change, checked pixel for pixel.** Before the change, six PDFs were generated with
  the real letterhead: the live-database sample invoices 447 and 448-FT-2026 (3 and 60 lines); the same
  two as unposted credit notes with discounts, notes, terms and bank details; a 45-row statement; and
  an empty statement. They were generated again after the change. All 12 pages, rendered at 100 dpi,
  are byte-identical.
- **Tests:** `src/lib/__tests__/a4Frame.test.ts` (7). A 120-row list has the frame, period, table
  header and correct `Page x of y` on every page; `ensureSpace` opens a framed page; empty seller
  parts are left out; file names; `printPdf` prints from the frame, downloads when printing throws,
  and downloads outside a browser. The existing `letterheadPdf.test.ts` (7) passes unchanged.
- **Checks:** `npm run lint`, `npm run test:unit` (16 files, 80 tests), `npm run build`,
  `npm run test:db` pass. `npm run bundle:check`: initial JS still 225.2 kB gzip; jsPDF, autotable
  and the frame are only in lazy chunks.
- **Not verified in the browser:** the invoice dialog and the Pending bills (PDF) button need a
  signed-in user. Nothing on screen changed in this stage (same PDFs, no button changed).

## Stage 2: Lists and reports

- **Every list and report PDF is now a letterhead document.** `exportToPDF` (`src/utils/export.ts`)
  kept its signature, so all callers changed at once: every `DataTable` (all list pages and every
  table in Reports) and the Trial Balance. It now builds the pages with `src/lib/a4/tableReport.ts`
  on the stage 1 frame:
  - top right: the report name and the period (`01-Jan-2026 to 15-Nov-2026`), or `As of <date>`
    for lists and current-state reports;
  - page 1: seller line (office VAT/CR, else the organization's), then Office (name and code, or All
    offices), Period / As of, Filters (search text, Trial Balance category and zero-balance choice),
    and "Generated by <user> on <date time>";
  - the table: brand header on every page, rows never split, figures right-aligned and never
    wrapped, totals row once at the end, "No rows" when empty;
  - bottom: `<report name> · Page x of y`.
- **Wide tables (decision 2).** Portrait unless there are more than 10 columns. The type is reduced
  from 8.5 pt until the columns fit (text columns count at most 45 mm and wrap), never below 6.5 pt.
  Above 10 columns: landscape A4 with the logo top left, a brand rule, and a footer line built from
  Settings (legal name, CR, VATIN, address, phone, email). The portrait letterhead image is not
  rotated or stretched. Long report names fit the corner beside the logo: one line down to 11 pt,
  then two lines at up to 10.5 pt (e.g. "VEHICLE MASTER / REPORT").
- **Which office and period.** Reports → each report: the office and From/To chosen on that page
  (`ReportExportContext`). Reports that show the current state (vehicle master, vehicle assets,
  depreciation, cost per km, tyres, reconciliation, AP and AR ageing) say "As of today", because
  their rows are not limited by the dates. Other list pages: the office chosen in the header.
  Trial Balance: "As of <its date>".
- **Company details** come from Settings (`OrgContext` → `setDocumentCompany`); the header office
  from `FleetContext` → `setReportOffice`. Nothing is typed in the code.
- **Document view → Print** now prints the same PDF pages as Download PDF (`printTablePdf` →
  `printPdf`), not the screen. Both buttons show as busy while the PDF is made; if the browser
  cannot print a PDF directly, it is downloaded and the dialog says so.
- **File names (B3):** `<Report>_<office code or All>_<from>_<to>.pdf`, or
  `<Report>_<office>_<date>.pdf` when there is no period (e.g.
  `Expense_Report_HO_2026-01-01_2026-11-15.pdf`, `Trial_Balance_All_2026-11-15.pdf`).
- **Tests:** `src/lib/__tests__/tableReport.test.ts` (8): period text; file names; landscape footer
  without empty parts; a 120-row list with title, period, header and page x of y on every page and
  the office/VAT/generated-by facts on page 1; a 9-column table stays portrait at ≥ 6.5 pt with its
  totals row; an 11-column table goes landscape with the company footer; an empty table prints its
  header and "No rows"; long names fit the corner on at most two lines. The old `pdfHeaderLines`
  test was replaced by these.
- **Rendered and checked** with the real letterhead: a 120-row, 7-column vehicle list (4 pages), a
  70-row trial balance with totals (3 pages, portrait, headings wrap), an 11-column asset report
  (landscape, logo and footer), an empty list. Invoices, notes and statements from stage 1 are
  still pixel-identical.
- **Checks:** lint, `test:unit` (17 files, 87 tests), build, `test:db` pass. Initial JS 225.4 kB
  gzip (+0.2 kB for the context and setters); jsPDF and the builders stay in lazy chunks. The running
  app loads with no console errors.
- **Not verified in the signed-in app:** the export menu, Document view and Trial Balance buttons
  with real data (no sign-in in the browser pane).

## Stage 3: Financial statements

- **New `src/lib/a4/statements.ts`**: pure functions that turn what each page already computed into
  letterhead table rows, so the paper shows the screen's figures. Figures use the screen's own
  formatter (`formatCurrency` without the currency prefix, which is in the column heading): same
  rounding, decimals and minus sign; costs and accumulated depreciation in brackets as on screen.
  - Profit & loss: Revenue, Direct operating costs, Gross profit (with margin), Operating expenses &
    depreciation, Net profit (with margin), the EBITDA note. Empty sections say so.
  - Balance sheet: the five sections with their totals, Total assets, Total liabilities & equity,
    and "OUT OF BALANCE by …" only when it is.
  - Cash flow: cash at start, the three activities with their net, cash at end, the note on what
    cash means.
  - General ledger: per account a heading row, opening balance, every line, and the account's
    activity totals with the closing balance.
  - Statement of account (also used in stage 5): opening, every movement with the running balance,
    closing with the period totals.
- **Group and total rows** in `tableReport.ts`: section headings span the table on a light brand
  tint; total rows are bold with a heavier rule above. Optional page-1 facts, signature lines
  ("Prepared by", "Approved by"), a fixed file name and office label.
- **Buttons.** New `PdfActions` (Download PDF and Print, busy while the PDF is made, a notice when
  the browser downloaded instead of printing, an error toast when it fails):
  - Profit & Loss: Download PDF and Print Statement (period from the page's dates).
  - Balance Sheet: Download PDF and Print Balance Sheet (as of the page's date).
  - Cash flow: Download PDF (needs the same export permission as CSV) and Print, for the office
    and dates chosen on the page.
  - Trial Balance: Print Statement now prints the PDF; figures use the screen formatter
    (thousands separators) and the PDF has signature lines.
  - General Ledger: on the ledger tab, Download PDF and Print fetch every line of every account in
    the range first (the export button shows the progress and can cancel); on the sub-ledger tab
    they make the party's statement of account (`Statement_<party>_<date>.pdf`).
  - Reports → Print Report prints the shown report's table PDF (the table registers itself with the
    page); it is disabled while a report has no table.
- **No `window.print()` is left** for these pages; `printElement` (unused) was removed. Still
  printing the screen: payment voucher and payslip (stage 4) and the invoice dialog's own Print.
- **Column widths:** figures columns keep their heading on one line when the table fits.
- **Tests:** `src/lib/__tests__/statements.test.ts` (9): screen-equal figures (OMR and USD, minus
  sign, no -0.000); P&L lines and totals on paper equal those passed in, with period and signatures;
  empty sections; balance sheet sections, brackets and out-of-balance line; cash flow nets; a
  3-account, 180-line ledger over several pages with the header and page x of y on each; statement
  of account rows and file names.
- **Rendered and checked** with the real letterhead and sample figures: P&L, balance sheet, cash
  flow, a 2-page general ledger (header repeated, empty account shown), statement of account.
  Invoices, notes and statements from stage 1 are still pixel-identical (12 pages).
- **Checks:** lint, `test:unit` (18 files, 96 tests), build, `test:db` pass. Initial JS 225.4 kB
  gzip; the statement builders load with the PDF code.
- **Not verified in the signed-in app:** the buttons with real ledger data (no sign-in in the
  browser pane).

## Stage 4: Receipts, vouchers and payslips

- **New `src/lib/a4/vouchers.ts`** (pure data functions plus builders on the frame):
  - **Receipt voucher** (a client's payment) and **payment voucher** (a payment to a supplier),
    numbered with the payment number: seller line (office VAT/CR, else the organization's),
    "Received from" / "Paid to" with the party, and Date, Office, Method, the bank account it went
    to or came from, Reference and Cheque no. (empty ones left out). One line "Received against
    invoice …" / "Paid against bill …" with the invoice date and total when the linked invoice
    could be read; the amount; the invoice balance due; amount in words; notes; "Received by" and
    "Authorised signature & stamp" (receipt) or "Prepared by" and "Receiver signature & date"
    (voucher). File `<payment number>.pdf`.
  - **Payslip**: title PAYSLIP with the period top right; the employee (ID, designation,
    department) and Period, Payment date (or scheduled), Status, Bank, Account, Days worked;
    earnings and deductions side by side with exactly the dialog's lines, order and totals; Net pay
    with the amount in words; "This is a computer-generated payslip."; "Prepared by (HR & Payroll)"
    and "Employee signature & date". **The bank account shows its last 4 characters only
    (`**** 4821`, decision 5).** File `Payslip_<employee code>_<yyyy-mm>.pdf`.
  - Amount in words only for OMR (the wording is Rials and Baisa); other currencies print the
    figure alone rather than wrong words.
- **Dialogs:** Payment detail → Download PDF and Print Receipt / Print Voucher. Payslip → Download
  PDF and Print Payslip. Both print the PDF. The payslip dialog no longer shows the invented
  initials badge.
- **Tests:** `src/lib/__tests__/vouchers.test.ts` (4): receipt facts, line, totals, words, file
  name and the drawn text; supplier voucher without a readable invoice and in USD (no words);
  payslip lines and totals equal the dialog's, the account masked and the full number nowhere in
  the PDF; masking of short and empty accounts.
- **Rendered and checked** with the real letterhead: receipt with a linked invoice, payment voucher
  by cheque without one, payslip with itemised allowances and deductions.
- **Checks:** lint, `test:unit` (19 files, 100 tests), build, `test:db` pass. Initial JS 226.7 kB
  gzip (+1.3 kB: the two dialogs are part of the app shell, so their Download/Print buttons are
  too); jsPDF and the builders stay in lazy chunks.
- **Not verified in the signed-in app:** both dialogs with real payments and payroll.

## Stage 5: Client and supplier statements

- **Reports → Client statement / Supplier statement:** Download PDF and Print statement next to
  "Pending bills (PDF)". The PDF is the STATEMENT OF ACCOUNT on the letterhead: top right the
  period chosen on the page; page 1 the seller line, Client (or Supplier), its address and VATIN
  when recorded, Office (the page's office, or All offices), Period and Generated by; the table
  Date, Reference, Detail, Charged / Billed, Received / credited (Paid / credited for suppliers),
  Balance; the opening balance on the first day of the period, every movement with the running
  balance, and the closing balance with the period totals. The rows are the ones the table on the
  page shows (same database statement, same opening and closing). File
  `Statement_<client>_<yyyy-mm-dd>.pdf` (the period's end date).
- **General Ledger → Supplier & Customer Sub-Ledgers** prints the same statement of account
  (stage 3), with its own column names.
- **"Pending bills (PDF)"** (bill-wise open invoices) is unchanged: it was already on the
  letterhead and is still pixel-identical.
- **Checked with live data** in a transaction that was rolled back: a Sample customer, two posted
  invoices of 1,942.500 and a payment of 1,000.000 against the first, then the database statement
  (`party_statement`) for 01-Jan-2026 to 15-Nov-2026. The PDF shows opening 0.000, the three lines,
  closing 2,885.000 and totals 3,885.000 / 1,000.000, equal to the database's `total_charge`,
  `total_credit` and `closing_balance`. The same payment printed as a receipt voucher (OMR One
  Thousand Rials, balance due 942.500). Nothing was kept: no Sample customer is left.
- **Checks:** lint, `test:unit` (19 files, 100 tests), build, `test:db` pass. Initial JS unchanged.
- **Not verified in the signed-in app:** the buttons on the Reports page.
